TrendDeck

Your data stays yours.

TrendDeck has no account, no server and no analytics. Everything it knows sits in one folder on your computer, and we never see any of it.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no tracking and no crash reporter. The app has nowhere to send your data.

Where your data lives

Brands, settings, logins, chats, drafts and media lists are files in the app's own folder on your computer (%APPDATA%\TrendDeck on Windows). Site logins are kept per brand, the same way a browser keeps them. Saved passwords are locked with your system keychain. Deleting a brand deletes its data, and deleting the folder deletes everything.

What goes out over the network

  • Sites you open. A site tab is a normal browser tab, so the site sees what it would in any browser.
  • Trends. The app asks Reddit, YouTube, Google Trends and TikTok for your keywords, from your own session.
  • Update check. The app asks GitHub for the latest version file. Nothing about you goes with it, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • Ad block lists. Public filter lists are downloaded about once a day.
  • Your AI agent. When you send a chat message, the agent you picked gets it and works through its own provider, under that provider's terms.

What AI agents can see

Agents only run when you send a message, and only for the brand that chat is for. They see pages only on sites you allowed. They never get cookies, saved logins, password fields or other brands, and they get nothing on sign-in pages. When an agent needs to sign in, the app fills the login itself and the agent only hears how it went. Before anything is posted, the app shows you the post on the site and you press Post yourself.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no forms. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which keeps standard server logs. The download button gets the installer from GitHub.

Questions

The app is open source, so every claim here can be checked in the code. Open an issue on GitHub for anything this page doesn't answer.
